# RailReady GK standard: evidence from RRB NTPC papers

Updated: 23 November 2026. Scope: RRB NTPC General Awareness, particularly CBT-1 Graduate standard.

## Sources examined

1. **Official RRB CEN 05/2024 NTPC Graduate notification**, section 13.1 (CBT-1 pattern and General Awareness syllabus). https://www.rrbcdg.gov.in/uploads/202405/Detailed%20CEN%2005-2024%20NTPC.pdf
2. **Published RRB NTPC 2016 General Awareness paper**, including world geography, first international cricket, science, history and culture questions. This is a third-party mirror of a paper, not a direct official answer-key verification. https://cdn1.byjus.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/11/RRB-NTPC-Previous-Year-Question-Paper-2016-General-Awareness.pdf
3. **Third-party 2025 Graduate General Awareness topic-wise compilation**, advertised as 24 shifts / 960 GA questions with answer keys. The compilation assigns chapters algorithmically and warns that categorisation may differ from the official section; its distributions are **indicative, not officially certified topic weights**. https://cdn.qmaths.in/pdfs/rrb-ntpc-graduate-2025/cbt1/topics/v1/RRB-NTPC-Graduate-2025-CBT1-EN-General-Awareness-TopicWise.pdf
4. **Published 20 August 2025 NTPC UG CBT-1 paper** as a supplementary illustration of international observances and Andes/South America questions; this is UG, not the Graduate paper. https://vault.sscdrishti.com/english_file_uploads/1767869455_RRB-NTPC-Under-Graduate-CBT-1-Question-Paper-English-20-08-2025-Shift-I.pdf

This is a **sample-based review**, not a systematic census of every official shift. No question in RailReady's new bank is represented as a verbatim PYQ.

## Official pattern

CBT-1: 100 questions in 90 minutes — 40 GA, 30 Mathematics, 30 Reasoning; −⅓ for an incorrect answer. CBT-2: 120 questions — 50 GA, 35 Mathematics, 35 Reasoning. The official GA syllabus explicitly includes both *India and World Geography*, *National and International Current Events*, *UN and other World Organizations*, *General Science/Life Science up to Class 10*, history, polity, environment, computers and economics.

## Examples informing the design

The 2016 sample includes the first international cricket match (USA–Canada), smallest continent (Australia), physical geography, wave frequency/wavelength and a basic chemistry fact. The 2025 third-party Graduate compilation includes large groups tagged as Indian Polity, Ancient/Medieval/Modern History, General Science, Computer Awareness and Geography. It also contains international current affairs, world geography and organisations.

### Findings / practical priorities

- **High priority:** Indian history and Constitution, general science up to Class 10, Indian and world geography, computer fundamentals. These should make up much of the static practice, using straightforward four-option facts and concepts.
- **Also important:** Economy, environment, world organisations and international events/sports. World GK must not be left out, but it should not replace Indian topics.
- **Targeted extra topic:** “First / Second / Second-last / Last” is useful for revision, especially clearly bounded historical sequences, but is not the principal question style across the sampled papers. For ongoing offices, last and second-last are undefined: do not generate such questions.
- **Separate current affairs:** A static GK bank cannot substitute for date-specific and fact-checked current affairs; keep national and international news preparation separate.

## New content and practice distribution

The World GK module adds **79 original concept/factual MCQs**, **28 original international chronology questions**, and **11 source-linked series**. It can also use the existing **73 original Indian GA questions** from the main study site for mixed practice.

The optional 40-question **static GA-only** drill targets this mix; it is **not an official mock paper**, and its 36-minute timer is a proportional practice aid, not a prescribed sectional time limit:

If a category lacks enough questions, the drill fills the remaining places with distinct questions from the available bank. The distribution is a **curriculum design choice** informed by the sample, not an assertion of exact RRB question weights.

## Limitations and next steps

The new GitHub Pages module and the original Floot-hosted Windows/Android wrapper are **separate applications**. Changes to GitHub Pages do not automatically update Floot. Integration into the original Floot GK tab requires a separate Floot edit and publish once build actions are available. The World GK page is available through the Complete Study web app after its GitHub Pages deployment succeeds.

The source materials should be periodically rechecked for exam-notification changes. Current affairs cannot be made accurate simply by inferring news facts from headlines.
