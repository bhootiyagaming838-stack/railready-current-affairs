How this bank was chosen

The official CEN 05/2024 syllabus covers Indian AND world geography, international current events, UN/world organisations, science, computers, history, economy, sports, and culture. Published 2016 and 2025 question-paper examples include direct factual recall, world places, basic science, shortcuts, and occasional international “first” records. This bank uses original questions following that style, not copied PYQs.
